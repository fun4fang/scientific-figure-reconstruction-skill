# Contributing to a research preview

Use synthetic, lawfully distributable fixtures. Do not submit paper images,
secrets, old Skill snapshots, font programs or unknown assets. Include origin,
license and relevant permission for new code/material. AI assistance does not
replace source/rights review.

Run `python -m unittest discover -s tests` after installing locked dependencies.
For output/editing changes, provide real native rendering and move/save/reopen
evidence as appropriate; serialized counts alone do not establish usability.
Keep scientific checks intact, preserve source meaning and document native vs
raster behavior. Do not tune on the consumed historical holdout or rewrite FAIL.
Any new paid model/upload test requires specific authorization.
